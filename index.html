Support centre

How can we help?

MkwanjaX presents football match reports, performance information and statistics using public data provided by PasuaX. Contact our team if you need help with the app or have a question about a report.

Email support

We usually reply within 24–48 hours, Monday to Friday.

Contact support

What can we help with?

Match report or statistics

Tell us the teams, competition and match date, and describe the information that looks incorrect or is missing.

App help or feedback

Share your device model, app version and the steps to reproduce the issue. Please do not include passwords or verification codes.

Privacy request

Email info@pasuax.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line.

About MkwanjaX

MkwanjaX is an informational football analytics app. Public report data is supplied by the PasuaX sports analytics and statistics service.

For faster help, include your app version, device model and a short description of the issue. Do not send payment details, passwords or one-time codes.
