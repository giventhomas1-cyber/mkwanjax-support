Privacy

MkwanjaX Analytics

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This policy applies to the MkwanjaX Analytics iOS app. The app displays public football match reports, fixture details and analyst profiles using the MkwanjaX public analytics service.

Information the app does not request

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. It does not include advertising or cross-app tracking features.

Technical request information

When the app requests a report over HTTPS, the service may receive and retain technical request logs, including the requested API path, request method, response status, request duration, IP address and app/device user-agent. These logs support service delivery, security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting. Because IP addresses and user-agent details can relate to a device or network, Apple’s privacy label identifies this category as Other Diagnostic Data linked to the user and used for App Functionality.

The app service uses rotating server logs; retention duration can vary with traffic and infrastructure. Hosting and network providers may also process technical connection logs under their own operational retention controls. The app does not use these details to track you across other companies’ apps or websites, to serve advertising, or to sell your personal information.

Public sports information

Match and analyst information shown in the app is public service content, not information submitted by your account. The app does not create a personal profile or store account details on your device.

Children

The App Store age rating can differ by country or region. The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who has a privacy concern can contact us below.

Privacy requests and contact

For questions or privacy requests about MkwanjaX Analytics, email info@pasuax.com with “MkwanjaX Analytics Privacy Request” in the subject line.

This policy is specific to the MkwanjaX Analytics iOS app. Other MkwanjaX website features may follow separate privacy terms.
